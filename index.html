What we install

One system for every kind of entrance

From apartment gates to factory shutters — every system is sized to the gate's weight and how often it runs in a day.

Sliding Gate Motors

Chain-driven operators for heavy track-mounted gates — the standard choice for apartment blocks and factory frontages.

Swing Gate Operators

Electromechanical arms for single or double hinged gates, with soft-start and soft-stop so the panel never slams.

Roller Gates & Shutters

Motorised roller shutters for shopfronts, warehouses, and garages, with manual override built in.

Smart Access Control

RFID cards, keypads, and app-based access, paired with multi-channel remotes for every household or vehicle.

How an installation runs

Four visits, start to finish

01

Site survey

We measure the gate's weight, check the power point, and note how many times a day it opens.

02

Motor & fitting

The right motor, track, and safety sensors are installed and aligned to the gate.

03

Programming

Remotes, keypads, or the app are paired, and the auto-reverse sensor is calibrated.

04

Handover & support

You get a walkthrough, warranty card, and a direct line to our service team.

Built for daily use

Why gates fail — and how we design around it

01

Auto-reverse safety sensors

Infrared beams stop and reverse the gate the instant they detect a person, vehicle, or pet in its path.

02

Battery backup

Every motor keeps working through a power cut, and can be released for manual push in an emergency.

03

Weatherproof housing

Sealed enclosures are rated for monsoon rain and coastal humidity, not just dry-season demos.

04

Local service, fast

Based in Kovilpatti, so a technician is never far from your site.
